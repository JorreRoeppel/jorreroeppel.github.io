• Classifying Breast Cancer Subtypes via Biologically Reasoned Feature Selection in Random Forest

In this project, we investigated how different feature-selection methods influence the classification of HER2+, HR+, and triple-negative breast cancer subtypes. Classification performance was evaluated using Random Forest models within a repeated nested cross-validation framework. The best-performing approach combined PAM50-overlapping regions with Elastic Net-selected features, achieving an accuracy of 0.84 ± 0.067.
